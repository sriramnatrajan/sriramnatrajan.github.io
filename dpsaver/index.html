Privacy policy

DP Saver (com.sriram.profiledpdownload). Effective 22 November 2026.

This notice explains what the Android app handles. It is not legal advice. DP Saver does not run its own accounts or its own servers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Instagram or Meta.

What the app does

You enter a public Instagram username or profile link, paste one, pick a recent search, or share a profile into the app. The lookup runs on this phone. The phone asks Instagram for that public profile photo and public details Instagram returns, such as the name, category, bio, verified badge, whether the account is private, and post, follower, and following counts. The app does not ask you to log in and does not see your Instagram password. It does not read an email address or phone number from the profile.

A private account can still show the public profile photo Instagram returns. The app does not open posts, stories, reels, or a private gallery.

On this phone

The app stores this on the device until you clear it, clear app storage, or uninstall:

  • Recent searches: username, display name, a small photo, the photo size, and the time.
  • Saved-photo list: username, where the file is in your gallery, size, quality, whether it is a favourite, and the time.
  • How many HD photos have been opened, and whether original quality was unlocked for a username for the rest of the local day.
  • Whether a Play review was already requested.

Photos you save go to Pictures/DP Saver. Those files stay in your gallery until you delete them. Clear recent searches and Remove saved photos are in Settings.

The lookup uses a hidden page load of instagram.com. Instagram may set cookies in that page on this phone. The app does not ask for a password. Clearing the app’s storage removes those cookies.

Paste reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. A profile shared from another app is used to fill the search box. That text is not sent to Firebase.

Android backup is allowed, so app preferences may be included in your Google account backup if backups are on. Gallery files are part of your device storage. We do not upload them to a DP Saver server.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a native ad and a banner on Home, a banner on the profile screen, a full-screen ad when you tap Download HD and sometimes when you leave a profile or when a rewarded ad is not ready, and a rewarded video from the second HD photo onward and before original quality. The first HD photo view does not ask for a rewarded video.

Google may use the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, and how you interact with an ad to choose the ad, limit how often it appears, and measure it. See Google’s privacy policy and how AdMob uses data. You can reset the advertising ID and limit ad personalization in Android settings. The app does not sell personal information.

Analytics and crashes

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records which screen you open and actions such as search, a profile result, download, share, copy link, set as profile photo, viewer quality, favourites, filters, clearing recents, removing saved photos, a rewarded video, a full-screen ad, a review prompt, and an app update. Those events use short labels such as “ok”, “fail”, “home”, or a quality name. They do not include the username, profile link, name, or bio.

Google may also receive an app instance ID, device and OS version, and general location such as country, under Firebase’s privacy information.

Crashlytics receives a crash stack trace, device model, and OS version so faults can be fixed. We do not add the Instagram username to a crash report. A technical log can still contain text that was on screen when the app crashed.

Google Play

After the first profile photo loads, the app may ask Google Play to show an in-app review, once. On each visit to Home, the app may ask Play if an immediate update is available. Play handles that request under Google’s own policy. Cancelling a required update closes the app.

What we do not collect

  • No DP Saver account, and no Instagram password.
  • No name, email, phone contacts, precise location, or payment details collected by us.
  • No username, profile link, bio, or saved photo uploaded to a DP Saver server or to Firebase.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, for the Instagram lookup, ads, analytics, and crash reports.
  • Storage on Android 9 and earlier, so a photo can be saved to the public Pictures folder. Newer versions save through the system photo library without that permission.
  • Advertising ID, which AdMob may use to choose and measure ads.

Children

DP Saver is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children under 13, or under the age required where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long it is kept

On the phone, until you clear it in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall, and subject to device backup. Gallery files remain until you delete them. Google keeps ads, analytics, and crash data under its own rules. We cannot look up an analytics record by your email, because we do not have accounts.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. The phone contacts Instagram for the public profile you request, and Google for ads, analytics, crash reports, and Play services. When you share a photo or link, or set a photo as a profile picture, the app you choose receives what you send. Information may also be shared if the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Continuing to use the app after that date means you accept the updated policy.
