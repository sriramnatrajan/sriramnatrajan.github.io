Privacy policy

This policy covers the InSaver Android app (package com.sriram.instasaver) and the KeepReel website. InSaver is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Effective 3 November 2026.

InSaver Android app

InSaver does not create an account and does not ask for your Instagram password.

When you paste or share a public Instagram link, the lookup runs on your phone. The link is sent to Instagram so the public media file can be found. InSaver does not send that link to a KeepReel server and does not keep a copy of it. Private posts and stories are not saved.

Videos, photos, and captions you save stay in the Downloads folder on your device. A screenshot you take in the player is saved to Pictures/InSaver on your device. Those files are not uploaded to us.

The app needs an internet connection for the lookup and for ads. On Android 9 and older it may ask for storage access so it can save files.

Advertising

InSaver shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, native ads, full-screen ads, and rewarded ads. Google may use the advertising ID, device information, IP address, and how you interact with ads to provide and measure advertising. Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. AdMob details are at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Analytics and crash reports

InSaver uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

Analytics receives app events such as sessions, the device model, the Android version, and an app instance ID. It does not receive the Instagram link you paste.

Crashlytics receives crash stack traces, the device model, and the Android version so faults can be fixed. It does not receive the Instagram link you paste.

Firebase’s policy is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details. We do not sell personal information.

Children

InSaver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long data is kept

InSaver does not store your links or downloads on a server. Google keeps advertising, analytics, and crash data under its own retention rules. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone’s settings, and you can uninstall the app to remove the files it saved on the device.

KeepReel website

On the website, a public link you submit is looked up by the KeepReel server so your browser can download the file. The server requests that public media from Instagram. The website does not ask for your Instagram password, does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact shown on the InSaver listing in Google Play.
